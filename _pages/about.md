I am a **Senior Computer Vision Engineer** at Magic Leap, passionate about bringing autonomous spatial intelligence and vision-based robotics to life.

In April 2023, I earned my **PhD in *robotics and computer vision* at the Vision for Robotics Lab (V4RL) at ETH Zürich** under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Margarita Chli. My research centered on active perception, path planning, 3D mapping, reinforcement learning, and multi-robot coordination. More broadly, my technical focus spans sensor fusion, Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO), Structure-from-Motion (SfM), and camera-based SLAM.

Prior to my PhD, I completed my **Master’s in *Robotics, Systems, and Control* at ETH Zürich** (2018) and a **Bachelor’s in *Mechanical Engineering* at Politecnico di Milano** (2015). My industry journey includes research internships at Magic Leap during my PhD and software engineering experience at Schindler Aufzüge AG.
