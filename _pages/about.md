I am a **Robotics & Computer Vision Engineer** on a mission to give machines full spatial autonomous intelligence. From tinkering with Arduinos to pushing autonomous drones through high-speed, GPS-denied environments at ETH Zürich, I’ve dedicated my career to bridging the gap between perception and physical action: what we now call **Physical AI**.

My technical foundation spans spatial computing, multi-agent coordination, and real-time state estimation across demanding hardware platforms.

Currently, I serve as a **Senior Robotics Engineer** at [Verity AG](https://www.verity.net/), bringing autonomous systems into high-impact real-world environments.

## 🚀 Industry Leadership & Spatial Computing
* **Verity AG** (*Senior Robotics Engineer*): Pushing the boundaries of autonomous drone systems and robotic intelligence in complex indoor operations.

* **Magic Leap** (*Senior Computer Vision Engineer*): Engineered high-performance C++/Python sensor fusion, Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO), and online calibration pipelines for next-generation AR/VR/XR headsets. Optimized SLAM estimation accuracy across complex edge cases, directly boosting tracking robustness and user experience against key KPI benchmarks.

    * **Google XR** (External Vendor via Magic Leap): Contributed advanced perception algorithms and spatial intelligence software for Google’s next-generation, AI-powered AR smart glasses.

* **Schindler Aufzüge AG** (*Software Engineer Intern*): Developed core software engineering solutions for physical industrial systems.

## 🎓 PhD & Research Roots
In April 2023, I earned my **PhD in Robotics & Computer Vision** from the **Vision for Robotics Lab (V4RL) at ETH Zürich**, supervised by Prof. Dr. Margarita Chli.

The focus of my dissertation was on *multi-robot coordination* and *semantic perception-aware active planning* for autonomous MAVs. My doctoral research pioneered how autonomous drones perceive, navigate, and land in extreme environments using semantic cues and deep Reinforcement Learning (RL).

### Core Technical Expertise
* **Spatial AI & Perception**: Visual-Inertial Odometry (VIO), Visual SLAM, Structure-from-Motion (SfM), Multi-Sensor Fusion, Online Calibration.

* **Autonomy & Control**: Active Perception, Semantic Path Planning, 3D Reconstruction/Mapping, Reinforcement Learning (Sim-to-Real), Multi-Agent Swarm Coordination.
